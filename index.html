The daily page

What happened today, centuries ago.

I.

On this day

Every date opens onto the same day across twenty centuries — a conversion on the Damascus road, a city fallen, a council convened, a hymn first sung.

II.

The whole story

Not a devotional, not a textbook. The saints and the schisms, the cathedrals and the controversies — history told with its politics and its poetry intact.

III.

No feed. No algorithm.

Nothing here is engineered to keep you scrolling. Read what catches you, follow it as deep as you like, and put it down.

History that rewards curiosity.

A blinding light on the road to Damascus. A vision before battle. A weeping scholar in a Milan garden. The walls of Constantinople breached after a fifty-three-day siege. Every card is a true story, told well, with the art to match.

And every story is a doorway — themes, scripture, people, and places, one tap deeper. Some days you read a card. Some days you fall down the rabbit hole. Both are the point.
